# MIRROR

### Your data remembers who you were.

MIRROR is a personal data application that transforms portable data into a living map of identity change.

It helps users discover behavioral contradictions, measure identity drift, explore how their digital behavior changes over time, and identify moments when significant behavioral shifts occurred.

MIRROR is designed around user-controlled data and consent.

---

## What Is MIRROR?

People change.

But most digital platforms only show who we are now.

MIRROR explores something different:

> **Who were you before, who are you becoming, and when did you change?**

By analyzing user-authorized personal data over time, MIRROR creates a historical view of behavioral patterns rather than a static personality profile.

---

## Core Features

### 1. Contradiction Engine

MIRROR identifies unexpected differences between behavioral patterns.

Instead of judging users, it highlights patterns that may reveal something they did not notice about themselves.

Example:

> You describe yourself as consistent.

MIRROR may discover:

> Your digital behavior shows a strong pattern of exploration and change.

The goal is self-discovery, not classification.

---

### 2. Data Time Machine

The Data Time Machine allows users to explore how their digital behavior changes over time.

Users can compare historical snapshots and observe changes in areas such as:

- Discovery
- Interests
- Content behavior
- Social behavior
- Stability
- Exploration

Example:

```text
January 2026
Exploration: 31%

April 2026
Exploration: 48%

August 2026
Exploration: 79%
```

---

### 3. Identity Drift

MIRROR measures how significantly a user's behavioral patterns have changed between two points in time.

Example:

```text
Identity Drift
78%
```

A high Identity Drift score does not mean that a person has become a different person.

It simply indicates that their observed digital behavior has changed significantly.

---

### 4. The Moment

MIRROR searches for significant points where behavioral patterns changed.

This becomes:

> **The Moment You Changed**

Example:

```text
Before
Exploration: 34%

        ↓

THE MOMENT
May 17, 2026

        ↓

After
Exploration: 71%
```

The feature helps users explore when a meaningful change began.

---

## Privacy First

MIRROR is built around user-controlled data.

The application should only access data that the user explicitly authorizes.

Users should be able to understand:

- What data is being accessed
- Why the data is needed
- How the data contributes to MIRROR
- Which permissions have been granted
- When access can be revoked

MIRROR does not aim to take ownership of a user's personal data.

> **Your data. Your permission. Your mirror.**

---

## Built for Vana

MIRROR is designed to use Vana's data portability and permission infrastructure.

The goal is to transform portable personal data into useful personal insights while preserving user control over the underlying data.

Vana provides the foundation for user-authorized data access, while MIRROR focuses on interpretation, behavioral analysis, historical comparison, and self-discovery.
